FinMate: Privacy Policy, Terms and Disclaimer

Privacy PolicyTerms & ConditionsFinancial Disclaimer

FinMate is a student project that helps people plan budgets. This page explains in plain language what the app really does with your information today.

Privacy Policy

1. What we collect

InformationWhy
Your name and emailTo create and identify your account.
Your password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in readable form)To let you sign in securely.
Financial details you type in: persona, funds, extra income, expenses, goals, budget settings and preferencesTo build your budget, dashboard, insights and plans.
Sign-in details: which device or browser, when you signed in, your network (IP) address, and failed sign-in attemptsTo show you your active sessions, protect your account from guessing attacks, and keep a security record.
The date you accepted these terms and which version you acceptedTo record your consent.
Whether you confirmed your email addressTo protect your account and let you reset a forgotten password.

We do not ask for bank passwords, card numbers, or government ID numbers. Please do not type them into FinMate.

2. How we use it

Only to run FinMate for you: to show your own numbers, calculate your plan, keep your account secure and let you manage your data. We do not sell your information and we do not show advertising. We send you email only for two reasons: to confirm your email address and to let you reset your password. We do not send marketing email.

3. Where it is stored

Your data is saved in the FinMate database on the server that runs the app. To make pages quick, your browser also keeps a copy of your saved entries and a sign-in token in its local storage. This is removed when you log out, and it is replaced by your account's data the next time you sign in.

4. Other companies' services

Some pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from public content networks (Tailwind CSS, Chart.js). Loading them means your browser contacts those services, which may see your IP address, as it would on any website. FinMate does not send your financial data to them.

5. How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting your account (Settings → Privacy & security) permanently removes your profile, funds, expenses, goals, settings, sessions and security records.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and copy: download everything we hold about you from Settings → Data export (JSON or CSV).
  • Correct: change your details and entries inside the app at any time.
  • Delete: delete your account and all its data from Settings.
  • Withdraw consent: stop using FinMate and delete your account.
  • Sign out other devices: from Settings → Privacy & security.

7. How we protect it

  • Passwords are hashed with a salted, slow algorithm and are never stored in readable form.
  • Every request is checked against your account, so other users cannot see your data.
  • Repeated wrong passwords temporarily lock sign-in attempts, and sign-in sessions can be ended from Settings.
  • Password reset and email confirmation links are single-use and expire quickly; only a scrambled version of each link code is stored. Changing your email address requires your password.
  • Security headers and input checks reduce the risk of common web attacks.

No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and keep it private.

8. Children

FinMate is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian's permission.

Financial Disclaimer

FinMate is an educational budgeting tool. It is not a Chartered Accountant, tax advisor, investment adviser or any regulated financial adviser. Its calculations, insights and predictions are estimates built from the numbers you enter. They are not financial advice, and planned amounts are not money you already have.
